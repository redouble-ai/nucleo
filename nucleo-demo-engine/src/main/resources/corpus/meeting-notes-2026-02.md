# Production meeting, 18 February 2026

Present: Jonas Berg (customer service), Mira Solheim (production), Tomas Reyes (sales),
Anke Vos (finance).

## Meridian 3 tooling

Mira reports the hydroforming die for the top tube passed its second trial at Tai Han
Alloy. First production frames ship week 14. The paint line in Kiel is booked from week
16, so the first complete bikes reach dealers in May, not April as the catalogue says.
Tomas will tell the twelve launch dealers this week, by phone, before the catalogue reaches
them.

Decision: the catalogue is not reprinted. A slip goes in the envelope.

Mira confirms the Meridian 2 is discontinued as of 31 December 2025; the last twelve frames
went to Pedalen in Copenhagen. Spare parts stay in stock for five years.

## Kestrel 1 pricing

Anke: Rimwerk raised the 29 inch wheelset from 180 to 205 euros in January, and the Kestrel 1
gravel carries two of them; the margin at 2,049 is thinner than on any other model.

Decision: Kestrel 1 gravel retail price 2,099 euros, dealer price 1,450 euros, effective
1 April 2026. Meridian 3 stays at 2,699 retail and 1,890 dealer for the year; Comet 2 stays
at 999. Tomas floats 2,799 for the Meridian 3 in 2027; not decided, revisit in October.

## Headset creak on early Meridian 2 frames

Jonas has 31 warranty claims since November, all on frames from the first two batches,
all the same creak from the lower headset bearing. The bearing supplier changed the seal
compound in October without telling us. Mira has the new seals in stock and every claim
is a bearing swap at the dealer, at our cost, roughly 40 euros each.

Decision: replace on claim, no recall. Jonas writes to every customer who has written to
us, including Ms. Aaltonen in Turku whose letter Anke flagged as unusually polite.

## Dealer margin

Anke: the 34 percent margin on stock orders over 20 units costs us more than the volume
brings on the Comet 2, whose list price leaves nothing to give. Proposal: the extra margin
applies to Meridian and Kestrel only. Tomas agrees if it is announced for 2027 and not
changed mid-season.

Decision: 2027 dealer terms, Comet excluded from the stock-order margin. Tomas drafts the
letter for the April dealer meeting.
