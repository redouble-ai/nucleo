## Aluminium surcharge

Anke: Tai Han Alloy has applied a raw material surcharge to every frame and fork shipped
after the April order, and Rimwerk's wheelset price is not coming back down. At the current
list we lose margin on every Meridian 3 and Kestrel 1 from the summer batch on.

Tomas: the dealers have had one change this year already, on the Kestrel. A second one
needs a full month's notice and a reason they can repeat to customers.

Decision: Meridian 3 and Kestrel 1 gravel retail and dealer prices go up by 20 percent from
1 June 2026, on the prices in force in May. The Comet 2 is not affected: it carries no
aluminium from Tai Han. Tune-up and bearing prices in the workshop are unchanged.

Decision: the 2027 Meridian 3 price discussed in February is off the table; the June change
replaces it.

## Actions

- Tomas: dealer letter with the June prices, out by 10 May.
- Anke: new price list on the portal, effective 1 June.
- Mira: confirm the surcharge in writing from Tai Han before the July order.
