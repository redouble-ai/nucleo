HALCYON BICYCLE WORKS - WARRANTY POLICY (2026 edition)

1. Frames
Every Halcyon frame is warranted to the first owner, for the life of the frame, against
defects in materials and workmanship. The warranty does not cover damage from crashes,
improper assembly, modification, or use in competition unless the frame is a model we sell
for competition. A warranted frame is repaired or replaced at our choice, with a frame of
the same or a later model.

2. Forks, paint and decals
Forks, paint and decals are warranted for two years from the date of purchase.

3. Bearings and wear parts
Headset, bottom bracket and hub bearings are warranted for one year. Tyres, tubes, chains,
cassettes, brake pads and cables are wear parts and are not warranted beyond what the law
requires.

4. How to claim
Bring the bicycle to the dealer you bought it from, with the warranty card or proof of
purchase. The dealer files the claim through the dealer portal. We answer within five
working days. Claims sent by email or post are forwarded to a dealer and take longer.

5. Transfer
The frame warranty does not transfer to a second owner. The two-year and one-year
warranties transfer with the bicycle.

6. What we ask of you
Keep the bicycle within the tyre pressures printed on the tyre, have the bearings serviced
yearly, and do not remove the frame number.

7. Out of warranty
A headset bearing replacement outside the warranty is EUR 40 at any Halcyon dealer, parts
and labour, at the 2026 rate.
